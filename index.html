DT Bytes 5-Point Survival Checklist

0 / 5 Ready
1. Keep Leverage Ultra-Low (1x–3x)Avoid forced liquidation during normal volatility and stop-loss sweeps.
2. Use Limit Orders for EntriesCapture lower maker fees (~0.02%) instead of market taker fees (~0.05%).
3. Top-Down Timeframe AlignmentMap primary trend and key support/resistance before 1m/5m entries.
4. Enforce a 30–120 Min Time BarrierExit stagnant trades within your planned window — avoid emotional holds.
5. Respect Daily Stop-Loss LimitsClose the terminal after hitting your daily max loss to stop revenge trading.
